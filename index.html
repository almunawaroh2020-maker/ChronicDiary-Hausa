Emergency information & safety

Emergency & Safety

Keep essential contact details within reach. ChronicDiary cannot identify or respond to emergencies for you.

Need urgent medical help?

For severe symptoms, serious breathing difficulties, chest pain, collapse, stroke-like symptoms, or a severe allergic reaction, contact your local emergency services or go to an emergency department immediately. Do not wait for a diary reminder.
